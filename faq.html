Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about our linen hire and delivery service. Can't find what you need? Email us.

Delivery & Coverage
Where do you deliver?
We deliver to properties in central Gold Coast from Broadbeach to Palm Beach. The exact coverage depends on the size of your order and whether you're a regular client. Contact us to confirm if we deliver to your location.
When do you deliver and pick up?
We deliver 5 days a week, Monday to Friday, between 10:00am and 1:30pm — timed to fit in between guest check-out and check-in. We can sometimes deliver earlier than 10am, but please contact us to confirm.
Do I have to be there when you deliver?
No, you don't need to be onsite. For Airbnb and holiday homes, we can leave the linen inside the gate or at the doorstep. For motels and resorts, we may require a fob for access. We do a 'swap over' — picking up used linen and dropping off a fresh batch at the same time.
Do you deliver to high-rise buildings?
We do not deliver to high-rise or low-rise buildings unless you are the onsite manager. If you place an order and pay for it before checking this, credit card and PayPal charges will not be refunded.
Can I pick up linen instead of having it delivered?
No, sorry — we only offer delivery. We do not have a pick-up service.
Ordering
Who do you deliver to?
We provide our linen hire and laundry service to: holiday home owners, housekeepers/cleaners, cleaning companies, Airbnb managers, real estate companies, resorts, and hotels.
Is there a minimum order?
Yes, there is a $100 minimum order on all orders.
Is there a delivery fee?
Yes, we charge a flat $14 fee to drop off your clean linen and pick up your dirty linen. This covers both the drop-off and pick-up in a single visit. There is also a 2% processing fee on all transactions.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and EFT. Please note there is a small 2% processing fee on all orders.
How much notice do I need before receiving linen?
New clients: Please allow at least 3 business days.
Existing clients: At least 2 business days (48 hours) for regular weekly deliveries.
For example, if you need delivery on a Monday, you must place your order by 10am the Thursday before.
How do I place an order?
Visit our Shop page, select your individual linen requirements, and check out. You must read and accept our Terms & Conditions before placing an order.
Linen & Quality
What items do you deliver?
We deliver fresh pressed, hotel-style commercial bed linen and amenities for your accommodation. We have 19 individual items available including bath towels, hand towels, bed sheets, pillowcases, bath mats, pool towels, tea towels, and more. View the full range on our Shop page.
Is your linen professionally cleaned?
Yes. All of our linen is professionally commercially laundered and pressed to be fully compliant with Australian Standards. You're getting the same quality used by leading hotels and resorts.
Can I wash the linen myself?
No. Linen must not be laundered in any form and must be returned for professional laundering only. Washing the linen yourself will damage the fabric and may result in replacement charges.
Returns & Hire Period
What is the maximum hire period?
The maximum hire period is 14 days. If you need linen for longer, you will need to re-order a changeover. Linen kept beyond 14 days without a re-order will be charged at 3 times the price of the linen not returned.
Am I penalised if linen isn't returned on time?
We need to receive linen back on the day your guests check out, as linen can turn mouldy if left too long. Extra charges may apply if linen is not returned on the agreed date. Soiled linen must not be held for more than 5 days.
How should I return dirty linen?
All dirty linen must be placed back in the linen bags provided, ready for collection. Linen bags must not be overfilled. When ordering online, the system calculates how many bags you will need to return the full order.
What happens if linen is lost or stolen by a guest?
You agree to monitor all linen and charge guests the replacement costs of any stolen or lost items. You must then notify us and forward the funds to us so we can replace the items.
