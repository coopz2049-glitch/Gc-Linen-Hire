Payment

  • We accept Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, and EFT.
  • Full payment must be received prior to orders being processed and delivered.
  • There is a 2% processing fee on all transactions.
  • There is a $100 minimum order on all orders.
  • You must be over 18 years of age to use this website.

Delivery & Fees

  • A $14 delivery fee applies to every order — this covers both drop-off of clean linen and pick-up of dirty linen in the same visit.
  • We deliver and pick up Monday–Friday, 10:00am–1:00pm.
  • If you are a regular client, you must have your next order placed ready for when we collect the dirty linen, so we can complete the swap-over.
  • We do not deliver to high-rise or low-rise buildings unless you are the onsite manager. If you place and pay for an order in breach of this, credit card and PayPal charges will not be refunded.
  • A signature may be required at the time of delivery.

Order Lead Times

  • Orders must be placed at least 48 business hours in advance to ensure stock availability for delivery, especially during peak periods. (Example: for Monday delivery, order by 10am the Thursday before.)
  • New clients require at least 3 business days notice.

Hire Period & Returns

  • The maximum hire period is 14 days. If you need linen for longer, you must re-order a changeover.
  • If linen is kept longer than 14 days without re-ordering, you will be charged 3 times the price of any linen not returned.
  • All dirty linen must be placed back in the linen bags provided, ready for collection. Linen bags must not be overfilled.
  • Soiled linen must not be held for more than 5 days, as it will go mouldy and be ruined. You will be responsible for replacement costs if this occurs.
  • We need to receive linen back on the day your guests check out. There may be extra charges if linen is not returned on the agreed date.

Linen Care

  • Linen must not be laundered in any form and must be returned for professional laundering only.
  • You agree not to stockpile linen and to only order what is required per stay, plus linen to cover weekend changeovers only.

Lost or Stolen Linen

  • You agree to monitor all linen and charge guests the replacement cost of any stolen or lost items.
  • You must notify us of any loss and forward the replacement funds to us so we can replace the items.

Pricing

  • We reserve the right to increase our prices at any time to cover CPI adjustments and laundry cost increases.
